Training Rules

Warm up

5–8 minutes of treadmill or cycle, then 1–2 light warm-up sets before your first major lift.

Progressive overload

When you reach the top of the rep range on every set with clean form, increase the load slightly next time.

Effort

For the first month, finish most working sets with roughly 2–3 good reps still available.

Recovery

Sleep 7–9 hours, walk daily, and keep at least one complete rest day each week.

Fat-loss checkpoint

Use your 7-day average weight. Only adjust calories after 2–3 weeks of consistent tracking.

Safety

Stop and seek medical advice for chest pain, fainting, unusual breathlessness or other concerning symptoms.
